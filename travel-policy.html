Travel Policy

FM Logistic Employee Travel Guidelines & Reimbursement Policy

Flight Policy

  • Economy class booking is applicable for all employees.
  • Business class requires Director/MD approval.
  • Bookings should be made through the approved travel desk.
  • Employees should choose the lowest logical fare available.

Hotel Policy

Employee Level Hotel Category Maximum Limit

Meal Allowance

Meal Type Maximum Limit
Breakfast ₹300

Local Transport Policy

  • Cab charges are reimbursable against valid bills.
  • Airport transfers are permitted for official travel.
  • Personal travel expenses are not reimbursable.
  • Ride-sharing options should be preferred whenever feasible.
